## Struktury-cele M2 (oś A) — rodziny

| Rodzina (id) | Co obejmuje | Przykład wymuszenia |
|---|---|---|
| `cleft` | It was X that… (zdania rozszczepione — CO było kluczowe) | „Of everything around that decision, what single thing actually tipped it?" |
| `only-inwersja` | Only then/after/when… did I… | „When did it finally feel real — was there a specific moment?" |
| `pytanie-zalezne` | asked whether… / I wonder whether… (pytania zależne) | „What do people around you keep asking about this whole plan?" |
| `hedging` | if anything / I'd argue / arguably (stanowisko z asekuracją) | „Some would say tutoring online is a crowded market — how do you see it?" |

W tle zawsze: rodzajniki `a/an/the/∅`, past simple vs present perfect.
Leksyka M2 do naturalnego recyklingu: make a call, get off the fence, go all
in, pull the trigger, set up (a channel), put together (a course), crank out
(videos), put it out there, walk (someone) through, come across as, think on
your feet, back up (with examples), hold (someone) accountable, slack off,
kid yourself, fall behind.
