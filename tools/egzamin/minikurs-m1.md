## Struktury-cele M1 (oś A) — rodziny

| Rodzina (id) | Co obejmuje | Przykład wymuszenia |
|---|---|---|
| `had-i-known` | inwersja warunkowa: Had I known/done… (bez „if") | „Looking back at leaving, what surprised you — anything you'd have played differently?" |
| `not-only` | inwersja: Not only am I…, I'm also… | „People think you just quit a job — is that the whole story?" |
| `what-cleft` | pseudo-cleft: What I can't figure out is… / What matters is… | „What's the hardest part to wrap your head around right now?" |
| `turns-out` | Turns out… (relacjonowanie zaskoczeń/odkryć) | „What did you expect about coming back that turned out different?" |

W tle zawsze: rodzajniki `a/an/the/∅`, past simple vs present perfect.
Leksyka M1 do naturalnego recyklingu: wrap up, head back, for good, mixed
feelings, on the fence, figure out, go out on my own, talk it over, sleep
on it, second-guess.
